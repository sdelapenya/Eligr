# Monetization

## Recommended Model

Freemium.

The free tier should make the core value obvious. Premium should unlock power, scale, and confidence.

## Free Tier

- 1 active search.
- Up to 5 rental options.
- Basic scoring.
- Basic comparison.
- Manual notes and visit checklist.
- Basic statuses.
- Share top 3 ranking and pair comparison.
- JSON backup export/import.

## Premium Tier

- Unlimited searches.
- More saved rental options.
- Advanced weighted scoring.
- AI-generated pros and cons.
- Anti-scam and risk analysis.
- Full ranking share.
- Export comparison PDF (planned).
- Collaboration with partner, friends, or family.
- Historical decision notes.
- Alerts and reminders.

## Possible Pricing

Initial price ideas:

- Monthly: 3.99 EUR to 5.99 EUR.
- One search pass: 2.99 EUR to 4.99 EUR for 30 days.

The one-search pass may fit the rental journey better than a permanent subscription, because many users need the app intensely for a short period.

## Best Early Monetization Bet

Use a hybrid:

- Free basic comparison.
- Premium "Rental Decision Pass" for one active search period.

Example:

> Unlock your search for 30 days: unlimited options, advanced scoring, anti-scam checks, visit notes, and shareable comparison.

## Future Revenue

- Affiliate deals with moving services.
- Tenant insurance partnerships.
- Internet/utilities setup.
- Paid verification reports.
- B2B tools for relocation agencies.

## Monetization Principles

- Do not block users before they understand the app's value.
- Keep the free tier useful but limited.
- Premium should reduce anxiety and save time.
- Avoid dark patterns around urgent housing decisions.
