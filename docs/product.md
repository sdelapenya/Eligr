# Product Vision

## Problem

Choosing a rental is messy. People compare rooms and flats across multiple portals, screenshots, WhatsApp conversations, notes, visits, prices, bills, deposits, locations, and vague impressions.

The real pain is not only finding rentals. It is deciding which option is actually best.

## Positioning

Eligr helps users save, compare, and choose rentals based on their own priorities.

It should feel like a calm decision assistant, not a noisy listing portal.

## Target Users

- Students comparing rooms near university.
- Young professionals moving to a new city.
- People comparing rooms, studios, or flats with tight budgets.
- Couples or friends deciding between several rentals.
- Expats or newcomers who need extra confidence before contacting or visiting.

## Core Value

Eligr answers:

> Which rental should I choose, and why?

The app should explain tradeoffs clearly:

- This option is cheaper, but has higher move-in cost.
- This option costs more, but saves commute time every day.
- This room has a good price, but several risk signals.
- This flat is the best match for your priorities.

## Product Principles

- Clarity over quantity.
- Comparison over endless browsing.
- Explainable scoring over black-box magic.
- Useful without scraping or owning supply.
- Monetization should enhance decision quality, not block the basic workflow.

## Brand Direction

Name: Eligr

Meaning: inspired by "elegir", focused on better decisions.

Tagline options:

- Compara alquileres. Decide mejor.
- Elige tu próximo alquiler con criterio.
- Compare rentals. Decide better.
